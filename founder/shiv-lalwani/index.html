Shiv Lalwani is the Founder and CEO of Virexa & Hartwell, an independent digital media and technology studio. The studio works across web design and development, UI/UX, digital products, software integrations, business automation and digital growth.

His focus is on building clear, useful digital experiences for businesses and founders, with a studio approach that brings strategy, design and engineering together.
