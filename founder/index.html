Founder & CEO

Shiv Lalwani is the founder and CEO of Virexa & Hartwell, a digital media and technology studio that designs and builds websites, product interfaces and growth systems for ambitious businesses.

About Shiv

Shiv founded Virexa & Hartwell with a simple idea: a business's digital presence should be useful, distinctive and technically sound — not just a template that exists. He leads the studio's direction across strategy, design, development and digital growth.

Under his leadership, Virexa & Hartwell works across web design, web development, UI/UX design, SaaS and product interfaces, API integrations and business automation, and SEO and digital growth.
