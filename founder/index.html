Shiv Lalwani

Owner · Founder & CEO

Shiv Lalwani is the owner, founder and CEO of Virexa & Hartwell — a digital media and technology studio based in Noida, India, that builds 3D websites, business websites, apps and growth systems for businesses.

About Shiv

Shiv founded Virexa & Hartwell in Noida with a simple idea: every business deserves a digital presence that looks premium, works properly and actually brings customers — not just a template that exists. As owner and CEO, he leads the studio's work end to end, from idea and design to development, launch and growth.

His work spans immersive 3D scroll websites, websites for local businesses like clinics, sweet shops, restaurants and stores, online shops, web apps and AI tools, along with automation, SEO and digital marketing that help those businesses grow.
