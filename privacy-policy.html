Trade with Arshi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trade with Arshi handles information submitted through its Meta (Facebook/Instagram) lead form for Demat account opening enquiries and related assistance.

Information We Collect

When you submit the lead form, we may collect the information you choose to provide, including your full name, phone number, email address (if requested), city and state.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information submitted through the form to contact you about the Demat account opening process, provide requested information or assistance, and follow up on your enquiry.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or other parties only when reasonably necessary to provide the requested assistance, operate the lead-management process, or comply with applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain submitted information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, legal obligations, and legitimate business record-keeping.

Your Choices

You may request correction or deletion of information you have submitted, subject to applicable legal requirements. For privacy questions or requests, please contact Trade with Arshi through its official Facebook page or the contact details provided by the business.

Meta Lead Forms

This policy applies to personal information collected through the Meta lead form used for Trade with Arshi's advertising and enquiry campaigns.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your submitted information, please contact Trade with Arshi through its official Facebook page or the contact details provided by the business.
