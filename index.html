06 / FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What is Virexa & Hartwell?

Virexa & Hartwell is a digital agency and technology studio focused on web design, web development, UI/UX, SEO, digital marketing, SaaS, API integrations and business automation for ambitious businesses and founders.

Do you work with US businesses?

Yes. Virexa & Hartwell is positioned for US businesses and works remotely with teams and founders worldwide.

How does a project start?

Send us the problem, the current website or product, and what you want to change. We respond with the next practical step.

Can you build beyond the visual design?

Yes. We can take a project from strategy and interface design through responsive frontend engineering, deployment and growth-focused landing systems.

Are the projects in this portfolio real clients?

The projects shown here are concept work created to demonstrate our range. They are not presented as client engagements or performance claims.

Can you work with an existing brand?

Yes. We can work from an existing identity or develop a new visual system when the current one is holding the experience back.
